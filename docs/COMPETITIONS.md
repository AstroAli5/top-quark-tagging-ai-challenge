## Student contribution and written materials

ISEF permits acknowledged AI use as a resource but requires independent student
work and student-authored material. Its current rules prohibit generative-AI
authorship of the research plan, abstract, poster and citations. This repository's
AI-generated technical protocols are development records; they are not approved
ISEF research plans. See [the assistance record](AI_ASSISTANCE.md).

An affiliated-fair continuation also needs a substantive new investigation;
merely repeating the same experiment with more samples is not sufficient under
ISEF's continuation rules. The appropriate student contribution and research
question must be reviewed before describing this as competition-ready.
